Help & Frequently Asked Questions

A quick guide to using YouBudget

Where is my information stored?

YouBudget is designed to keep your imported statements and planning data locally in your browser. Clearing site data may remove them.

Do I need to connect a bank?

No. You can enter planned flows manually and, in Advanced mode, import supported spreadsheet statements.

What is the difference between Basic and Advanced?

Basic focuses on planned cash flows and forecasting. Advanced adds imported transactions, spending insights, categorization and additional scenarios.

Are savings estimates guaranteed?

No. Savings opportunities and recurring suggestions are hypothetical estimates based on available transactions and may overlap with reductions already planned.

Does changing currency convert my money?

No. The currency preference controls denomination and formatting; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

How can I change the language?

Use Language & region in Settings. Some translations are still being developed; English is the fallback.
