Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Local-first financial information

YouBudget is designed to process your manually entered financial information and imported statements in your browser. The current web application does not require an account or transmit your statement data to a YouBudget server. Data stored in your browser may be lost if you clear browser storage, change devices or use private browsing.

What the site may process

Public website hosting and infrastructure providers may receive standard technical information such as IP addresses, device information and request logs. YouBudget does not currently run AdSense advertising scripts in this release.

Advertising and cookies

Public pages include reserved ad locations, but no live ads are loaded. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process browsing data for ad delivery, measurement and personalization, subject to applicable law and user choices. We will update this notice and implement applicable consent controls before enabling advertising.

Financial data and advertising

Financial statements, balances, transactions, merchant names and forecast calculations must not be sent to advertising services or used for ad targeting. Advertising will be restricted to public informational pages.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored YouBudget information using the application’s available controls or your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing browser data may permanently delete your saved plans. Browser settings can also limit third-party cookies.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. A working contact address and any required business identity details must be supplied before public launch. This policy should be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which YouBudget is offered.
