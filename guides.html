Budgeting Guides

Practical ways to plan ahead

1. Build a cash-flow baseline

Start with a realistic opening balance. Enter predictable income and bills, and review their expected dates. A simple forecast is often more useful than a detailed but incomplete plan.

2. Look ahead for tight months

Review projected balances over the next three, six and twelve months. Identify periods when expenses arrive before income and consider timing adjustments or a cash buffer.

3. Review recurring payments

Inspect subscriptions and regular bills. Distinguish essential commitments from discretionary spending before testing hypothetical reductions.

4. Compare scenarios carefully

Test one change at a time, then combinations. Avoid counting the same saving twice if a reduction is already included in your plan. Treat all results as estimates.

5. Keep your data accurate

Update your plan as bills, income and spending change. If importing statements, review category assignments and transfers between your own accounts.
